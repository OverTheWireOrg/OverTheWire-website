Discord-IRC bridge: Terms of Service
====================================

*Last updated: 2026-11-10*

The OverTheWire community runs a bot that connects (bridges) the channels on our
[Discord server][chat] to the matching channels on our [IRC server][chat]. The bot is based on
the open-source [discord-irc][] software. These Terms of Service apply to everyone who sends or
receives messages through this bridge, including Discord users and IRC users.

Please also read the [Privacy Policy][privacy] for the bridge, which explains which data the
bot handles and why.

1. Accepting these terms
------------------------
By chatting in a bridged channel on Discord or IRC, you agree to these terms, to the
OverTheWire [rules][], and to the Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the
bridged channels.

On Discord you must also follow the [Discord Terms of Service][discord-tos] and
[Discord Community Guidelines][discord-guidelines]. This includes Discord's minimum age
requirement.

2. What the bridge does
-----------------------
- Messages sent in a bridged Discord channel are copied to the matching IRC channel. Messages
  sent in a bridged IRC channel are copied to the matching Discord channel.
- Each copied message includes your Discord display name or IRC nickname, so others can
  see who wrote it.
- Attachments posted on Discord, such as images and files, are not uploaded to IRC. The bridge
  posts a link to the attachment on Discord's servers instead.
- If you edit or delete a message on Discord after sending it, the copy on IRC does not change.
  Messages that have already been copied cannot be taken back.
- Some Discord features do not exist on IRC, such as spoiler tags, embeds, reactions, threads
  and formatting. They may not be shown or may look different on IRC. For this reason, the
  [rules][] say that you may not rely on Discord's `/spoiler` feature.
- The bot is only for the OverTheWire Discord server and is
  [not available for public installation][install].

3. Your conduct
---------------
When you use the bridge, you must not:

- break the OverTheWire [rules][], such as by posting spoilers, game credentials, spam or
  harassment;
- post anything illegal, or anything that infringes someone else's rights;
- abuse the bridge, for example by flooding it, trying to make it relay commands, or
  pretending to be another user, a staff member or the bot itself;
- try to disrupt, overload or attack the bot, the IRC server or the Discord server;
- post personal information about other people without their consent.

4. Your content
---------------
You keep ownership of what you post. By posting in a bridged channel, you allow OverTheWire to
relay your message, together with your display name or nickname, to the other platform, where
it is shown to other participants. You are responsible for what you post.

Remember that bridged channels are public. Anyone who joins the Discord server or connects to
the IRC server can read the messages. Other users and their clients may also keep logs, and
OverTheWire has no control over that.

5. Moderation
-------------
OverTheWire staff and moderators may remove messages, mute, kick or ban users on Discord and/or
IRC, or stop relaying messages from a user, if they think the user is breaking these terms or
the rules. They may do this at any time and without notice.

6. Availability and changes
---------------------------
Volunteers run the bridge free of charge, "as is", without any guarantee. Messages may arrive
late, be relayed incompletely, or be lost. The bridge may be changed, interrupted or shut down
at any time.

To the extent permitted by law, OverTheWire and its volunteers are not liable for any damage
resulting from the use of the bridge or from content posted by other users.

We may update these terms from time to time. Changes are published on this page, and the
"Last updated" date above is changed. If you keep using the bridge after a change, you accept
the new terms.

7. Contact
----------
If you have questions about these terms, please contact the OverTheWire staff using the
methods described on the [Contact via Chat][chat] page.
